Privacy Policy

Effective: July 21, 2026

1. Who we are & scope

Linx Photos provides photo tools and hosting (including InstaLay and public portfolio hosting components). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use our websites and related features.

2. Information we collect

  • Account & identity data: name, email address, and authentication details.
  • Content you submit: photos, albums, captions, and other materials you upload or share.
  • Communication data: messages you send to support or submit legal requests.
  • Usage data: pages viewed, actions taken, and diagnostic/error information.
  • Device & network data: approximate location from IP, and browser/device characteristics.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: identifiers used to provide features and remember preferences.

3. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the services.
  • To manage accounts, authentication, and security.
  • To host, store, process, and deliver your content.
  • To process support and legal requests.
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and security incidents.

4. When we share information

We share information only when necessary to operate the services, comply with law, or protect rights and safety. Examples include service providers acting on our behalf and disclosures required by law.

5. Data security

We use safeguards designed to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention

We retain information as long as necessary to provide the services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

7. Your choices & rights

You may be able to request access or deletion of certain information by contacting support. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings.

Contact: support@linx.photos.

8. International transfers

We may process information in countries other than where you live. When required, we take steps intended to provide appropriate safeguards.

9. Children's privacy

The services are not intended for children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date indicates when changes became effective.
